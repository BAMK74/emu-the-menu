# Open Food Facts for barcode lookup

A barcode is turned into a product name and Pack size by one read-only request to Open Food Facts, a free and open product database that needs no account. Commercial barcode databases were rejected because they need paid keys. Open Food Facts is crowd-sourced and some products have no pack size, so every result is shown for confirmation and never written to Stock on its own. Only the barcode leaves the house: no Household data or country is sent, because a barcode identifies a product worldwide. Requests carry a custom User-Agent and stay well under the published limit of 15 reads per minute per IP, and found products are cached in the database so each barcode is fetched once. If the service is unreachable the form says so and quantities are entered by hand. The data is licensed under the ODbL, so the page credits Open Food Facts whenever it shows a looked-up product.
