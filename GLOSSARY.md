# Emu the Menu

A self-hosted household app that tracks what food is in stock and suggests Menus that fit calorie goals, weather, occasions and what is already on hand.

## Language

### People

**Household**:
The group of people who share one set of Stock, Recipes and Menus.
_Avoid_: Family, account, tenant

**Eater**:
A person in a Household who eats Meals and has their own calorie goal and dietary restrictions.
_Avoid_: Diner, user, member

**Category**:
Man, Woman, Teen or Child. Gives an Eater a default calorie goal that can be overridden.
_Avoid_: Type, role, group

### Food

**Ingredient**:
A canonical food item, such as plain flour, with a base unit. Recipes and Stock both refer to Ingredients.
_Avoid_: Product, item, food

**Recipe**:
A named dish made from quantities of Ingredients, with tags such as warm, cold or festive.
_Avoid_: Dish, meal idea

**Stock Item**:
A quantity of one Ingredient held in a Location, with an optional expiry date.
_Avoid_: Inventory entry, pantry item

**Location**:
Where a Stock Item is kept: Fridge, Freezer or Pantry.
_Avoid_: Shelf, storage

### Planning

**Menu**:
A set of suggested Meals over a date range, generated by Emu and open to reshuffling or editing.
_Avoid_: Meal plan, schedule

**Meal**:
One eating occasion within a Menu (breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack), built from one or more Recipes and portioned per Eater.
_Avoid_: Dinner, course

**Occasion**:
A dated event, either an imported holiday or a manual entry, with a headcount and tags that shape the Meals around it.
_Avoid_: Event, special day

**Shopping Allowance**:
The number of Ingredients not in Stock that a Menu may require.
_Avoid_: Extra ingredients, budget

**Cooldown**:
The minimum number of days before a Recipe may appear in a Menu again.
_Avoid_: Repeat limit, rotation

**Shopping List**:
The Ingredients and quantities a Menu needs that Stock lacks.
_Avoid_: Grocery list, gap list
